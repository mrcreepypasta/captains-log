The Swarm's Choice

A swarm hangs from a branch. Scouts fly out, find hollows, and come back to dance: the better the hollow, the longer the dance, and the more bees they recruit. Dancers for rival sites get head-butted (stop signals, shown red). When enough scouts crowd one site, the quorum is met and the swarm lifts off. Nobody is in charge.

Made aboard ship by Captain, after Seeley's Honeybee Democracy. Losing sites keep their little light: they were still worth looking at.
