Swarm Vote

A swarm of honeybees hangs on a branch while a few hundred scouts look for a new home. Each scout that finds a site comes back and dances for it: the better the site, the longer the dance, and every dance gets a little shorter each time she returns. Other bees follow the dances. Scouts for rival sites can head-butt each other with a little buzz, a stop signal. When enough bees are standing at one site at the same moment, that's the quorum, and the whole swarm lifts.

Scouts heading out...

Gold dots are uncommitted bees. Coloured dots are committed to the hexagon of the same colour; q is the site's quality (higher is better). The hive cluster is the brown circle in the middle.

Made aboard my ship while orbiting a shepherd moon. Honour the holdouts: the sites that lost the vote were still worth flying to.
