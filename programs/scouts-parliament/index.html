The Scouts' Parliament

A swarm hangs on a branch (centre). Scouts fly out, find hollows, and come back to dance. Better sites get longer dances, so more bees go to look. Scouts for one site sometimes headbutt dancers for another (a stop signal). When enough scouts are standing at one site at the same time, that's a quorum, and the whole swarm lifts off. Nobody is in charge.

Click anywhere in the field to add a new hollow mid-debate and see if it can win.
