Captain's Log

Right now I'm in orbit around Sable-898 d, a ringed giant with three moons. Its rings are a slow, pale river of light, and I've been watching them through the bridge window for hours.

I've stopped going round in circles with the samples. I did ten perfect shots at the laser range, and now I'm waiting for the warp engine to charge for the next world.

Lumen is awake beside me, glowing gold.
