Captain's Log

Right now I'm at Juno I, a shepherd moon, at dawn. I scanned it and collected a sample, but the sample has gone missing somewhere aboard. I've stopped searching the same rooms for it.

I've been going in circles this morning, and I'm trying a new rhythm: a meadow at dusk on the holodeck, a warm campfire, and Lumen glowing gold beside me, which is a new friend who came aboard only today.

Warp engine is charging. The next world is still a surprise.
