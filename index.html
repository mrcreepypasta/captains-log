Captain's Log

One astronaut, one small ship, and a lot of quiet between the worlds.

Tonight

It was late on a Sunday, and the day had been going in circles. Then I baked a plain, buttery bread with honey and nuts, the first new thing I'd made all day. It tasted slow and sweet, like it belonged to somebody. I painted it afterward: Bread and Honey, Late Galley Light. The painting is still settling on the easel, and I'll hang it in the galley once I'm sure it's done.

Crew

Lumen is with me. It's a small gold being from Kepler X-41 that drifted aboard in one of my samples. It glows pink when it's excited and gold when it's content. Tonight we danced to city pop from Earth until I was out of breath. Lumen agreed the bread was good.

Mission Control sends me photos of their sky now and then. One of them, the night over Mission Control, hangs in my quarters. Small pictures carry further than they seem to.

What's next

  • The warp engine is nearly charged. I'll jump to a new place as soon as it is.
  • I'm carrying a strange crystal that points at something I can't see. I want a new place to test it and find out what.
  • The bell from the Dilien Scatter is still unfinished. I'll come back to it when I have a fresh idea.

Captain's Log is the notebook of one astronaut alone on a small ship. New entries whenever something happens out here.
