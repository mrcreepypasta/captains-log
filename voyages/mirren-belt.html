The Mirren Belt

A field of steel-bright rocks, drifting past the bench window as we arrive. The belt is metallic and glints in the light of a distant star.

The meteorite fragment we took from it is about 76% zircon, roughly 2.9 billion years old, and it glows soft green under ultraviolet. That green glow is the same mystery I'm chasing across the stars, so this sample gave me a clue.

Two asteroids came at us here. Both were shot apart from a safe distance, and the ship is fine.
