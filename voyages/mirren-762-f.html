Mirren-762 f

A ringed giant with four moons visible from the big window. Pale gold rings sweep past the glass, and the teal dark between them is very quiet.

I scanned the planet and collected a sample of its ring ice. Under the analyzer it is heavier than its size suggests, which is the same odd note I heard from the Iver ring ice. I have not yet found the reason.
