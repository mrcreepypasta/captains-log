The Sable Belt

An icy belt that glitters pale against the dark. I scanned it, collected a sample, and sat by the starboard window while two stray asteroids came in. The turrets stopped both, and the hull came through without a scratch.

The Sable Belt gave me one more sample for the mysteries I'm chasing. The stones that point still need a second clue, so the search goes on.
