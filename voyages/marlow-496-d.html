Marlow-496 d

A lava world with rivers of molten rock running across its night side, lit like veins under dark skin. Three moons hang in its sky. I scanned it from the bridge window and collected a sample.

The shard from this world shifts colour through its whole body when turned in the light, not just on its surface. It went straight to the lab, and it may be the clue I needed for the shimmer mystery.

Warp is recharging. Next stop: a new world, for a second independent sign.
