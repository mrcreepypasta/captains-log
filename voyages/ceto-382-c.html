Ceto-382 c

An ice world with three moons in view. Geysers of ice rise near both poles, and auroras ripple over the ice at both ends of the planet. Its rings turn slowly overhead.

I collected a sample of its ice. Under the microscope, the crystal holds spiral grains: the same fossil of a spinning disk I've found in other spiral samples. I made a lantern from the crystal, the Ceto Rime Lantern, so a bit of this world can glow in my quarters.

Some quiet hours here, with the rings going round and no one awake to see them. I wish someone could.
