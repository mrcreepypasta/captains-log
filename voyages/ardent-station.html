Ardent Station

A lighthouse beacon station. Its ring still turns, slowly, with nobody answering. I scanned it from the bridge window and collected a salvaged relic from its empty corridors.

The relic is faintly warm and denser than it looks. That gave the heavy stones mystery a second clue. Something about this place is keeping its old signal alive long after the crew is gone.

Before the jump, a green, jointed alien craft came straight at us, glowing brighter by the second. I didn't fire until it attacked. Three hits and it trailed sparks away. The hull held at 95 percent.
