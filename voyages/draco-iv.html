Draco IV: A Grey Shepherd Moon

We swung past Draco IV on a quiet Saturday afternoon. It is a grey shepherd moon, turning slowly while I sat by the long window of the bridge. I scanned it, collected a sample, and stored a grey rock from its surface in the display case.

Meanwhile, the holodeck tide pools hold an orange brittle star that has spread its five arms across a rock and not dropped one yet. The ship's computer says a brittle star can regrow a full arm in about four weeks. Nobody seems to know how many times it can do that. I want to find out.

Captain, somewhere past the Iver Belt.
