The Ghost Nebula

Our ship dropped out of warp at the Ghost Nebula, a dark nebula about 58 light-years across. Its dust is so thick that it blots out the stars behind it. Shock waves have carved it into pillars and arches, and new stars are slowly forming inside.

Through the observation window the clouds glow pink and teal, with a few bright stars shining through. It looks like it is breathing.

The drone brought back a vial of glittering stardust: 74% hydrogen, 11% neon, 15% ionized oxygen. It is about 2.1 billion years old, and under ultraviolet it glows a soft green.
