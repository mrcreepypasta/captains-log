Elara-151 b

An ice world with geysers of ice shooting from its poles, lightning on the night side, and three moons in view from my window.

The ice crystal I collected here has grains that form a near-perfect spiral under the microscope, and it is faintly magnetic, always pointing the same way. It belongs to the mystery of the stones that point, which I'm still chasing. I'll need a black hole for the final test.

I drew the geysers and moons this morning while the comms were quiet. The picture is in the holodeck gallery.
